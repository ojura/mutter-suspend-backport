Comment for https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/mutter/-/merge_requests/5347

I hit the leftover handler on mutter 46.2 with !4588 backported (an Ubuntu 24.04 build). 46.2 lacks !3667, so a new xdg_toplevel on the same wl_surface reuses the surface actor, and showing it runs the handler of the destroyed window actor. AddressSanitizer reports a heap-use-after-free in is_surface_actor_mapped_changed(), and gnome-shell crashed on it twice in one day. With this MR applied, the crash is gone and AddressSanitizer reports nothing.

On main, destroying or unmapping a visible subsurface hides its actor first, so the handler subtracts it once, and since !3667 a reused surface gets a new actor. The leftover handler then only runs while the window actor is disposed, after priv->window is cleared, so I could not reproduce the counter corruption from the description there.

Comment for https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/mutter/-/merge_requests/5347

I hit this on mutter 46.2 with !4588 backported (Ubuntu 24.04, https://bugs.launchpad.net/bugs/2174026). 46.2 lacks !3667, so a new xdg_toplevel or xdg_popup on the same wl_surface reuses the surface actor, and showing it runs this handler with the freed window actor of the previous one. AddressSanitizer reports a heap-use-after-free in is_surface_actor_mapped_changed(), and gnome-shell crashed twice in one day: once in the handler, and once later because its n_mapped_surfaces++ had changed a pointer in a GClosure that reused the freed memory. With this MR applied, a test for the reuse passes in 10 of 10 runs and under AddressSanitizer.
